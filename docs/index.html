    Dae-Jin Lee

    Assistant Professor in Statistical Sciences

    IE University

    I am assistant professor (tenure-track) in Statistical Sciences and researcher at the School of Science & Technology at IE University.

    Before joining IE University, from 2014 to 2022, I was a researcher at BCAM – Basque Center for Applied Mathematics in Bilbao, where I led the research line in Applied Statistics and coordinated the area of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence of the Knowledge Transfer Unit.

    From 2011 to 2014, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at CSIRO (Mathematics, Informatics and Statistics; today Data61) in Melbourne, Australia.

    My research focuses on biostatistics and statistical modelling for health, biomedical, environmental, and sports-science applications—especially smoothing methods based on penalized splines and mixed models.

    Interests
    • Generalized Additive Models (P-splines)
    • (Non-)linear mixed-effects models
    • Longitudinal data analysis
    • Survival analysis
    • Spatial and spatio-temporal modelling
    • Sports analytics
