**Central question.** How can shape-constrained and nonlinear mixed models support reference-to-individual growth prediction, maturation timing (APHV), and athletic ageing trajectories?

**Methods.** I/M-splines and monotone P-splines; SITAR-type growth models; derivative estimation; hierarchical / mixed-effects frameworks.

**Why it matters.** In youth sport and paediatric health, decisions depend on *where someone is on their developmental curve*—not only on calendar age. In adult athletes, the same trajectory language helps describe decline, recovery, and long-term capacity.

**Selected evidence.** Work on peak height velocity and injury burden in youth football; derivative-curve estimation in longitudinal studies; ongoing methods for shape-constrained growth curves and athletic-ageing envelopes.
