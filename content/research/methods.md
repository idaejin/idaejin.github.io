**Central question.** How do we make flexible longitudinal methods *computationally and scientifically reusable*—across PRO/survival problems, multidimensional smoothing, and applied pipelines?

**Methods.** Multidimensional and adaptive P-splines; beta-binomial and related models for patient-reported outcomes; joint modelling of longitudinal PRO and survival; TMB / Template Model Builder implementations; open software for applied workflows.

**Why it matters.** Themes 1–3 share a methodological core. Packaging that core as engines and tools is what lets the same ideas travel from youth growth curves to COPD patient-reported outcomes to athlete monitoring.

**Selected evidence.** Multidimensional adaptive P-splines; beta-binomial mixed models for longitudinal PROs; joint PRO–survival analysis in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; knowledge-transfer and software-oriented projects.
