Open tools are part of how the methodological work travels.

- **[`injurytools`](https://lzumeta.github.io/injurytools/)** — R toolkit for sports injury and illness data analysis ([CRAN](https://cran.r-project.org/package=injurytools); [GitHub](https://github.com/lzumeta/injurytools)). Developed with Lore Zumeta Olaskoaga; supports preparation, description, and modelling workflows for recurrent injury settings.
- **[`PROregTMB`](https://github.com/idaejin/PROregTMB)** — Template Model Builder implementations connected to beta-binomial / PRO modelling lines.
